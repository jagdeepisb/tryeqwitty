Executive assessment

Qualified · Withhold a valuation range. Two customers paid, but their contract terms and recurring billings are unknown. Total annual revenue cannot serve as contracted recurring revenue.

Reported revenue of USD 100,000 less USD 40,000 in direct costs gives a 60% gross margin (E1).
