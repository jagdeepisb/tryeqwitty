# Changelog
## Unreleased — audit corrections

Clarifies published-release pinning, rejects dirty tracked source checkouts when establishing conformance provenance, and prevents an export from mixing newly generated files with existing report artifacts. Adds regression tests. The immutable v2.0.0 release is unchanged.

## 2.0.0 — stable standard and release conformance

Pins the analytical, financial, research, reporting and evidence contracts in a release profile. Adds analytical-bundle and reconciled-report checks, compatibility and migration guidance, and an evidence package with reproducible reports, benchmark gates and source and artifact hashes. Records maintainer-approved MIT and CC BY 4.0 licensing, contribution procedures and governance. [Release profile](release/v2.0.0/README.md)

## 1.5 — benchmarks and independent cross-agent evaluation

Adds closed, synthetic and public corporate-record cases with independently checked keys, raw trial outputs, a blinded review rubric and reproducible scores. The pilot runs fixed-evidence and live-retrieval tracks with separate denominators and stated limits. Adds seeded failure-gate tests and report-to-bundle reconciliation before rendering. The underlying analytical bundle remains v1.4.

## 1.4 — research playbooks and adaptive depth

Adds mandatory factors routed by achieved stage, decision objective and business model, with evidence requirements and disconfirming tests. Adds a question graph, expected-information priorities, research allowances, actual attempt records, distinct-origin corroboration and correction routes. The validator and report now expose actual scope and material unresolved questions. Two synthetic cases demonstrate differentiated research and unavailable tool disclosure. Prior bundle versions retain their validation and rendering behaviour.

## 1.3 — structured PDF reporting and financial exhibits

Adds the structured company and competitor matrices, claim-level source links, financial tables and charts, scenario schedule appendix, DCF sensitivity grids and tagged PDF output. The HTML export includes the exact-value and operand registers. Complete and sparse examples include PDF, HTML and missing-information CSVs. CI verifies numeric reconciliation, source links, PDF text and the embedded brand fonts. v1.0 report JSON and older analysis bundles remain supported.

## 1.2 — advanced financial modelling and valuation

Adds typed, sourced financial inputs, stage and model eligibility, linked monthly liquidity scenarios, priced primary equity and dilution, and an annual FCFF DCF with WACC, equity bridge and sensitivity. Adds a versioned contract, complete and sparse fixtures, and known-answer and adversarial tests. The validator accepts v1.1 bundles and both versions feed the portable v1.0 report renderer.

## 1.1 — proposed release
Detailed execution gates, evidence rules, public model-specific factor library, financial/valuation guides, structured analysis bundle, deterministic checks, compatibility bridge, synthetic benchmarks, tests and CI. Original v1.0 report renderer remains supported. No hosted app scoring, live benchmark or exact report parity claim. Licensing remains to be explicitly selected by the maintainer.
