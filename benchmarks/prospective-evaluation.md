# Requirements for the next prospective evaluation

The November 2026 pilot is an archived repeatability measurement for one inherited Codex configuration. It does not establish generalisation, cross-model superiority, or expert-validated investment judgment. Its thresholds were chosen after observing results. Do not alter the frozen pilot to improve appearances.

Before any new solver receives a case, publish a dated registration containing the objective, eligible metrics and denominators, pass thresholds, sample design, provider configurations, budgets, failure rules, reviewer criteria and analysis plan. Commit the registration and freeze its hash. Record deviations separately; do not change thresholds after observing outcomes.

## Minimum design for a broader claim

- Recruit at least two qualified, identifiable human financial or business diligence reviewers, with relevant experience and conflicts disclosed. AI-assisted review may supplement, not replace, their judgments.
- Commission an independent case curator to retain genuinely unseen inputs and keys outside solver-accessible repositories until outputs are locked. Obtain documented permission for nonpublic records. Do not call publicly committed cases an unseen holdout.
- Include difficult cases with conflicting records, missing data, mixed business models, cash and ownership complications, misleading claims, unusable comparables, and legitimate grounds to withhold an estimate. Include public or permissioned real-company evidence and at least one live retrieval task beyond registry metadata.
- Register multiple provider configurations if accessible. Use equivalent inputs, tool allowances and planned repeats. Compare the standard against a predeclared baseline prompt, not only against itself.
- Record every planned run, unsuccessful attempt, source open, model identifier when exposed, and reviewer disagreement. Mask provider identity during grading. Retain original submissions and record the source of each score.
- Report case-level results, denominator, failure categories, reviewer disagreement and uncertainty. Assess confidence on both correct and incorrect answers where observed; if errors are absent, say calibration on errors remains unmeasured.

Proposed planning floors (not a registered or completed campaign): 95% factual and arithmetic accuracy, 90% contradiction recall, zero fabricated sources or unsupported material valuations, and no concealed critical blocker. A future dated registration must lock the actual thresholds before trials. A small sample does not warrant population-wide accuracy claims.

## Publication status

No new holdout has been commissioned, no human expert adjudication has been obtained, and no cross-provider or baseline comparison has been performed under this plan. A real-company assessment may be published only from authorised or public materials, with an explicit review status. Do not label an AI-only assessment independently expert-reviewed.
